Our commitment to your privacy

Privacy Policy

ss+ Creative Studio is a creative media venture connected to Skilled Services Plus (ss+). This policy explains how we handle information that visitors choose to share through this website.

Information we collect

When you send an inquiry, we collect the details you enter: first name, last name, email address, phone number, and message. The form also uses a technical request identifier to help confirm whether a message was received and to prevent duplicate records.

How we use it

We use your information to read and respond to inquiries, discuss possible collaborations, and maintain appropriate correspondence. We do not use your inquiry details for unrelated marketing without an appropriate basis or further permission.

Where inquiries are stored

Our contact form uses Google Apps Script to route submissions to the same restricted Google Sheets inquiries tracker used by Skilled Services Plus. Authorized people supporting the ss+ ventures may access those submissions to respond to inquiries. Google's services process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

Third-party services and media

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages and may display original images and video. Standard technical information, such as browser requests, may be processed by the hosting provider. If additional external media services are added, this policy should be reviewed accordingly.

Requests and questions

If you have questions about information you submitted or want to request its correction or removal, please contact the studio. Requests are considered in light of applicable requirements and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

Updates

We may update this policy when our site, services, or data practices change. The current version will be posted on this page.
